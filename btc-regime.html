      How to read this

      Five states. A state is reached only when every one of its conditions is true — one failing condition is enough to rule it out.

      Early uptrend Long-favourable Price has reclaimed its one-year average and is climbing without leverage driving it — funding is below median and open interest is flat or falling. The least crowded conditions for holding a long, and therefore the ones with the most room left. Also the ones most likely to be a failed reclaim, because nothing has confirmed yet. Requires: above 200DMA · spot-led · funding below median · structure not rolled over
      Confirmed uptrend Long-favourable Everything agrees. The average is rising, weekly swings are stepping higher, and open interest is growing while funding stays short of extremes — new money entering an intact trend. This is the state where trend-continuation is best supported, and the answer to "can the uptrend continue" is most clearly yes. Requires: above a rising 200DMA · weekly HH/HL · OI expanding · funding not extreme
      Late uptrend Long, but fragile Still an uptrend, but longs are paying near the top of their two-year range and leverage is piling in fast. Crowding says a reversal would be violent, not that one is due — crowded markets can stay crowded for weeks. Read this as a position-sizing and stop-placement condition rather than a reason to flip short. Requires: above 200DMA · price still up over 20d · funding at/above the 85th percentile · OI up more than 10%
      Downtrend Short-favourable Price is below its one-year average and at least one of the two confirmations agrees — either that average is falling, or weekly structure has rolled over to lower highs and lower lows. Rallies inside this state are counter-trend until one of those conditions changes. Requires: below 200DMA · plus falling 200DMA or weekly LH/LL
      Conflicted No directional edge No state has all its conditions met — trend, structure and positioning point different ways. This is a finding, not a failure to decide. The disagreement is printed at the top of the page, and the conditions table shows exactly which checks would have to flip to resolve it. Averaging the disagreement into a single score would hide the one thing worth knowing. Requires: nothing — it is what remains when no other state qualifies

      What this is. A state description of Binance BTCUSDT spot and perpetual data, recomputed from live public endpoints. It classifies the present; it does not forecast, target a price, or tell you what to trade. Every threshold below is a choice, not a validated parameter — they are printed in the conditions table so you can edit them in this file.

      Machine-readable snapshot. /api/snapshot returns the state, the tier verdict and every indicator as JSON, recomputed server-side on request. Set a SNAPSHOT_TOKEN environment variable on the project to require a read-only token. Thresholds come from config.json .

      Known limits. Single-venue perpetuals are a biased sample of total market positioning, not the aggregate. Open interest history is capped at 30 days by the public endpoint, so the OI-to-volume figure is a 30-day range position, not a 2-year percentile; it is divided by a trailing 7-day mean volume because raw daily perp volume has a strong weekday cycle that would otherwise dominate the reading. Funding percentiles use a trailing 2-year window against an asset with roughly 4-year cycles, so the same absolute condition ranks differently depending on where the window sits. Equity proxy leadership (COIN, MSTR, miners) is absent: no free browser-reachable source sends CORS headers. Nothing here is filled in, interpolated, or estimated — failed requests grey their panel and report the status code.
